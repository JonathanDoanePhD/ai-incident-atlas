# Tag propagation to uncoded incidents (kNN)

_Generated by `python -m atlas.analysis.propagate`._

Incidents not coded by the LLM (792) receive **predicted** tags from their 15 most similar coded incidents (cosine similarity of title + AIID description embeddings; similarity-weighted votes; a multi-label term is kept when its vote share ≥ 0.4). Predicted tags are labelled as such in the app and are excluded from the evidence-based statistics.
